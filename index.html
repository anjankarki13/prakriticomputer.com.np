Questions

Frequently asked

What does the 24-hour service guarantee mean?
We commit to completing standard repair and service jobs within 24 hours of you handing the device over. If a job needs a part we do not have in stock, or turns out to be more serious, we tell you that up front rather than letting it drift.
Do you charge for checking a device?
Basic diagnosis at the shop is free. If a fault needs deep hardware testing we will tell you the checking charge in advance, and it is adjusted against the repair cost if you go ahead.
Will I lose my data during a repair?
We take a backup of your important files before any repair or software installation whenever the storage is readable. Please tell us if you have data you cannot afford to lose so we can prioritise it.
Do you visit offices and schools?
Yes. We provide on-site installation, networking, CCTV and annual maintenance (AMC) for offices, schools, shops and institutions across the district. Call 9824186811 to arrange a visit.
Do you sell second-hand computers?
We deal mainly in new and branded machines, and we also help you upgrade your existing computer instead of replacing it — often a cheaper and better option. Ask us what makes sense for your budget.
What are your opening hours?
Sunday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on Saturdays and public holidays. For urgent service you can still call 9801444271.

Need help with a computer today?

Call us, message us on social media, or drop by the shop. We will tell you honestly what your device needs — even if the answer is "nothing".
