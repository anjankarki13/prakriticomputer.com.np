About Prakriti Computer

A local shop, a familiar face, and technology help you do not need a dictionary to understand.

Our story

Built on one simple idea — tell people the truth

Prakriti Computer began as a small counter serving neighbours who needed a printout, a repair, or advice on which computer to buy. Very little of that has changed. We are bigger now, we carry more, and we handle work for offices and institutions — but the reason people keep coming back is the same one they came for on the first day.

Technology is confusing enough without a shopkeeper making it worse. So we explain what is actually wrong with your device, tell you when a repair is not worth the money, and never sell you a bigger machine than your work requires. Sometimes that costs us a sale. It has also given us customers who have been with us for over a decade.

What we stand for

The promises behind every job

These are not slogans on a wall. They are the rules we run the shop by.

A price before the work

You approve the cost before we begin. If we discover something else along the way, we stop and call you first.

Your data is private

We back up what matters before we start, and nothing from your device is copied, kept or shared afterwards.

Time you can plan around

We tell you when your device will be ready — and if a part has to come from outside, you hear that immediately.

Repair before replace

If your machine can be upgraded instead of replaced, we will recommend that — even though it earns us less.

Support that continues

Call us six months later with a question about something we sold you. We will still help — that is part of the deal.
