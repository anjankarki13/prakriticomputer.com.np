Our Services

Eleven services under one roof — whether you need a new laptop, a printer that has stopped feeding paper, a CCTV system for your shop, or someone to keep your office computers healthy all year, we can help.

Computer & Laptop Sales

Branded laptops and desktops plus custom-assembled PCs. Tell us your budget and what you will use it for — study, office work, accounting, design or gaming — and we will recommend the right machine instead of the most expensive one.

Laptops Desktops Custom builds Monitors & UPS

Laptop & Desktop Repair

Machine not starting, restarting on its own, overheating or crawling slowly? We diagnose the real fault — motherboard, power supply, display, storage or software — and tell you the cost before we touch anything.

No power / dead board Screen replacement Keyboard & battery Cleaning & servicing

Hardware Upgrades

An old computer is often three years away from retirement, not three days. An SSD and a little more RAM can make a slow machine feel brand new for a fraction of the price of replacing it.

SSD & hard disk RAM upgrade Graphics card Power supply

Printer Sales, Service & Refilling

Inkjet, laser and dot-matrix printers supplied, installed and repaired. We also refill cartridges and stock toner, so your billing counter or office never stops mid-day.

Cartridge refill Toner supply Head cleaning Paper feed repair

CCTV Camera Installation

We survey your premises, advise how many cameras you genuinely need and where, then supply and install the full system — including setting up live viewing on your mobile phone before we leave.

HD & IP cameras DVR / NVR Mobile viewing Servicing

Networking & Internet Setup

Wi-Fi that reaches the back room and a network that lets every computer share one printer and one internet line. We handle cabling, routers, switches and the dead spots nobody else could fix.

LAN cabling Router setup Wi-Fi extension Printer sharing

Software, OS & Antivirus

Fresh Windows installation with all the correct drivers, essential office software, and genuine antivirus protection — set up properly so your machine stays fast and safe.

Windows install Drivers Office suite Antivirus

Data Recovery & Backup

Deleted photos, a formatted drive, a hard disk that no longer shows up — bring it in before you try anything else. We also set up simple backup routines so it never happens twice.

Deleted file recovery Formatted drives Pen drive & memory card Backup setup

Billing & Accounting Software

Installation, configuration and training for the billing and inventory software your business runs on — plus help at year end, during audits and when the software refuses to cooperate.

Installation Company setup Staff training Ongoing support

Accessories & Consumables

Everything that goes around the computer, in stock and reasonably priced — so you are not hunting three shops for a cable on a working day.

Keyboard & mouse Pen drives & hard disks Cables & converters Headphones & webcams

Office IT Support & AMC

Annual maintenance contracts for offices, schools, hospitals and shops. Scheduled servicing, priority response when something breaks, and one number to call instead of five.

Annual contracts On-site visits Priority response Institutions welcome
